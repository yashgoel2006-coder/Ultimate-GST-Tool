Every sheet from B2B to the right in the portal file (B2B, B2BA, B2B-CDNR, B2B-CDNRA, ECO, ISD, IMPG, ITC Reversal, Rejected sheets, etc.) becomes its own tab, with all months stacked one below the other and a Month column added (e.g. May-25). Figures are copied exactly as in the portal file and no column is dropped (where the portal changed a sheet's columns during the year, columns are matched by heading). The tabs use the GST portal's look. Extra tabs: Checks, Completeness (every non-empty source cell and column heading checked month by month in each sheet) and Tie-out (each month's sheet totals against the 2B's own ITC summary).
