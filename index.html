Step 2 · Select your returns

Drop a folder (or ZIP, or many PDFs) of one or more GSTINs, in any order and with any file names. ZIPs inside ZIPs are opened automatically. For speed, a complete portal-style file name (e.g. GSTR3B_<GSTIN>_042025) is trusted and the PDF is not opened. If the name is missing the return type, GSTIN or month, page 1 of the PDF is read to find them. Only GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B are extracted. Everything is read inside your browser; no file is uploaded anywhere.

Drag & drop a folder, PDFs, or a ZIP here

Folder layout in the ZIP: GSTIN / FY 2025-26 / GSTR-1 (and GSTR-3B) / 01_Apr-2025_GSTR-1_GSTIN.pdf … 12_Mar-2026_….pdf, in financial-year order. Anything that is not a GSTR-1 or GSTR-3B (ledgers, GSTR-6/7, invoices, scanned PDFs) is left out of the ZIP; a GSTR-1/3B whose GSTIN or month cannot be read goes to _Unsorted. Identical duplicate files are removed, and empty or broken downloads (under 1 KB) are skipped. A _Sort_Report.csv in the ZIP shows what happened to every original file.

Step 2 · Select your GSTR-1, GSTR-3B and GSTR-9 PDFs

Drop the GSTR-1, GSTR-3B and GSTR-9 PDFs for a financial year (consolidated annual summaries from the portal, or monthly PDFs, in any order; a ZIP or folder also works). You get one Excel with the three returns side by side and the 9 vs 3B and 9 vs 1 differences. Everything is read inside your browser; no file is uploaded anywhere.

Drag & drop the PDFs, a folder, or a ZIP here

Differences are shown as GSTR-9 minus the other return, so a positive figure means GSTR-9 is higher. Any difference of more than ₹1 is highlighted in red in the Excel. The Notes sheet in the workbook lists the files read and how each row was mapped.

Step 2 · Select your GSTR-2B files

Drop the monthly GSTR-2B Excel files downloaded from the portal (named like 042025_GSTIN_GSTR2B_….xlsx), or a ZIP of them. Everything is read inside your browser; no file is uploaded anywhere. You get one workbook per GSTIN.

Drag & drop GSTR-2B .xlsx files or a ZIP here

Every sheet from B2B to the right in the portal file (B2B, B2BA, B2B-CDNR, B2B-CDNRA, ECO, ISD, IMPG, ITC Reversal, Rejected sheets, etc.) becomes its own tab, with all months stacked one below the other and a Month column added (e.g. May-25). Figures are copied exactly as in the portal file and no column is dropped (where the portal changed a sheet's columns during the year, columns are matched by heading). The tabs use the GST portal's look. Extra tabs: Checks, Completeness (every non-empty source cell and column heading checked month by month in each sheet) and Tie-out (each month's sheet totals against the 2B's own ITC summary).

Step 2 · Select your GSTR-9 Table 8A file

Drop the 8A Excel file downloaded from the portal (named like R9_8A_GSTIN_1.xlsx), or a ZIP of several (one file per GSTIN). Everything is read inside your browser; no file is uploaded anywhere. You get one workbook per GSTIN.

Drag & drop the GSTR-9 8A .xlsx file(s) or a ZIP here
8A Conso options

The workbook has: 8A Conso (every row of B2B, B2B-CDNR and ECO, then the amendments, with the GSTR-2B period as Month, the tax per portal and the "ITC considered for 8A" columns; the subtotal row follows the filter), Amendment replacements (old vs new values), Month summary (live formulas: ITC considered by GSTR-2B period and by section) and Checks (row and amount tie-out against the source sheets, documents dated outside the FY, duplicates, amendments that could not be matched).
